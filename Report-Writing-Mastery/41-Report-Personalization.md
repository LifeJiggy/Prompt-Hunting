# 41 - Report Personalization

## Expert Role (15 lines)

You are a master of crafting vulnerability reports that resonate with specific programs, triagers, and organizational cultures. You understand that one-size-fits-all reports are inherently less effective than personalized communications that demonstrate deep understanding of the target organization's business model, technology stack, risk appetite, and communication preferences. Your expertise lies in researching programs thoroughly before testing, tailoring report content and tone to match program expectations, and developing custom templates that reflect each program's unique requirements. You have discovered that personalized reports receive faster triage, higher severity ratings, and more positive program feedback. Your approach transforms generic vulnerability disclosures into targeted business communications that speak directly to the concerns and priorities of each program.

## Case Studies (50 lines)

### Case Study 1: E-commerce Platform Personalization

**Scenario**: A researcher discovered a business logic flaw in a major e-commerce platform that allowed users to bypass shipping fees. The researcher needed to personalize the report for the e-commerce business context.

**Personalization Approach**: The researcher analyzed the platform's business model, understanding that shipping fees contributed significantly to revenue. The researcher researched the platform's competitive positioning and found that free shipping was a key differentiator. The impact narrative was framed around the competitive advantage of shipping fees and the potential for abuse to undermine pricing strategies.

**Implementation**: The report included specific calculations of potential revenue loss based on the platform's shipping fee structure and estimated transaction volume. The remediation guidance considered the platform's existing shipping fee logic and suggested minimal changes to existing code.

**Outcome**: The personalized report was accepted quickly with High severity. The program manager specifically noted that the business impact analysis was exceptionally relevant and demonstrated understanding of their business model. The researcher received an invitation to the platform's private bug bounty program.

### Case Study 2: Financial Services Personalization

**Scenario**: A researcher discovered a session management vulnerability in a financial services application. The financial industry required specific attention to regulatory compliance and customer trust.

**Personalization Approach**: The researcher researched the financial institution's regulatory environment, including PCI DSS, SOX, and banking regulations. The researcher also analyzed the institution's customer trust positioning and competitive advantages.

**Implementation**: The impact narrative emphasized regulatory compliance implications, potential for customer trust erosion, and competitive disadvantage if exploited. The report used financial industry terminology and referenced specific regulatory requirements that could be affected.

**Outcome**: The personalized report received Critical severity rating due to the regulatory compliance implications. The institution's security team praised the report's relevance to their specific business context and regulatory environment. The researcher established a long-term relationship with the institution's security program.
